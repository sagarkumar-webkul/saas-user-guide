# Registration & Sign-in

Before a merchant can sell, they join the platform and get in. Registration takes three short steps, after which the storefront and admin panel are provisioned automatically.

::: info What you'll learn
- How a merchant registers a store in three steps
- How the store is provisioned
- How an existing merchant signs in
:::

## Registering a store

Open the platform address and go to the registration page. Registration runs in three steps, shown as **01 Your Sign**, **02 Account**, and **03 You**. The store's address is reserved at the first step.

**Step 1 — Your Sign.** Enter the **Store name** and a **Username**. As you type the username, the subdomain your store will live at is previewed below it. Select **Reserve & continue**.

<ImagePopup src="/images/registration/tenant-register-1.png" alt="Registration step 1 — store name and username" />

**Step 2 — Account.** Enter the **Email**, **Password**, and **Confirm password** the store will sign in with. Select **Continue**.

<ImagePopup src="/images/registration/tenant-register-2.png" alt="Registration step 2 — account details" />

**Step 3 — You.** Enter the owner's **First name**, **Last name**, and **Phone**. Select **Open your store** to finish.

<ImagePopup src="/images/registration/tenant-register-3.png" alt="Registration step 3 — personal details" />

::: info The store is set up for you
Once registration finishes, the storefront and admin panel are created automatically. There is nothing to install — the tenant can sign in and begin adding products right away. See [Store & Admin Panel](/tenants/store-management).
:::

## Signing in

A returning merchant goes to the **Sign in** page and enters their **Email**. The platform looks up their store and redirects them to sign in at their own store's address.

<ImagePopup src="/images/registration/tenant-sign-in.png" alt="Find your store sign-in" />

They can also go straight to their store's admin login at **their-domain/admin/login**.

<ImagePopup src="/images/registration/tenant-admin-login.png" alt="Tenant admin login" />

::: tip
Each store signs in at its own address, not a shared one. If a merchant isn't sure of theirs, the **Sign in** page finds it from their email and sends them to the right door.
:::
