# Dashboard & Sign-in

The Super Admin panel is where the whole platform is run. This page covers getting in and reading the dashboard that greets you.

::: info What you'll learn
- How to sign in to the Super Admin panel
- What the dashboard tells you at a glance
:::

## Signing in

Open the platform address and go to **/super/login**, then enter your registered **Email Address** and **Password** and select **Sign In**.

<ImagePopup src="/images/dashboard/super-login.png" alt="Super Admin Sign In screen" />

If you forget your password, use **Forget Password?** to receive a reset link by email.

## The dashboard

After you sign in, the **Dashboard** opens with an overview of the whole platform.

<ImagePopup src="/images/dashboard/super-dashboard.png" alt="Super Admin dashboard" />

At a glance it shows:

- **Overall Details** — total and active tenants, active subscriptions, and total revenue.
- **Recent Tenants** — the latest stores to join, with their subdomain and join date.
- **Recent Invoices** — the most recent subscription invoices and their amounts.
- **Subscription Stats** and **Plan Distribution** — signups over time and how tenants are spread across your plans.
- **Expiring Plans** — subscriptions approaching their end date.

::: tip
The dashboard is a read-only summary. To act on what you see — approve a plan, edit a tenant, chase an invoice — jump to [Tenant Management](/super-admin/tenant-management) or [Purchased Plans & Invoices](/subscriptions/purchased-plans).
:::
