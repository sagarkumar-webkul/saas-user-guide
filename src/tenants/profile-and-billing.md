# Profile & Billing

A merchant keeps their own contact details and billing address up to date from the Tenant Admin panel.

::: info What you'll learn
- How a merchant edits their company profile
- Where the billing address used on invoices is managed
:::

## Company profile

Under **Settings**, a merchant opens **Edit Company Detail** to update their profile.

<ImagePopup src="/images/profile-and-billing/tenant-profile.png" alt="Edit company detail" />

They can change:

- **First Name** and **Last Name**
- **Email**
- **Skype**
- **cName** — the custom domain to point at the store (see [CNAME Mapping](/domains/cname-mapping))
- **Phone**

Select **Save Detail** to keep the changes, or **Back** to return.

## Billing address

The billing address used on subscription invoices is managed under **Billing → Billing Address**. Select **Add Address** to add one, entering the address lines, city, state, country, postcode, and phone.

<ImagePopup src="/images/profile-and-billing/tenant-billing-address.png" alt="Billing address" />

::: tip
Add a billing address before subscribing to a paid plan — it appears on every subscription invoice, so getting it right once saves correcting invoices later. See [Tenant Billing](/subscriptions/tenant-billing).
:::
