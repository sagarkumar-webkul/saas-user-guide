# Purchased Plans & Invoices

Once tenants start subscribing, you track who is on which plan and what they have been billed. This page covers the super admin's view of active subscriptions and their invoices.

::: info What you'll learn
- How to see which tenants are on which plan
- Where to find subscription invoices
- How to assign or cancel a tenant's plan
:::

## Purchased Plans

Go to **Subscription → Purchased Plans** to see which tenants are on which plan, the amount, the billing period, and the profile state.

<ImagePopup src="/images/purchased-plans/super-purchased-plans.png" alt="Purchased plans" />

Click the **View** (eye) icon on a row to see the full subscription profile for that tenant.

## Subscription Invoices

Go to **Subscription → Invoices** to see every invoice raised for a subscription, with the customer, total, expiry, and creation date. Click the **View** icon to open an individual invoice.

<ImagePopup src="/images/purchased-plans/super-invoices.png" alt="Subscription invoices" />

## Assigning or cancelling a plan

From a tenant's [insights page](/super-admin/tenant-management#tenant-insights) you can **Assign** a plan to a tenant directly, or **Cancel Plan** to stop their current subscription.

::: tip
Use **Assign** to grandfather a tenant onto a specific plan without making them go through checkout — handy for migrations, trials, and negotiated deals.
:::
