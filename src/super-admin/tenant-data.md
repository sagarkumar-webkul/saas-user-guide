# Tenant Data

The super admin can review the data behind every tenant store — their customers, products, and orders — in one place. These views are read-only: they let you audit and support without touching a merchant's store.

::: info What you'll learn
- Where to see all tenants' customers, products, and orders
- Why these lists are read-only
:::

## Customers

Go to **Tenants → Customers** to see every customer across all tenant stores.

<ImagePopup src="/images/tenant-data/super-customers.png" alt="All tenant customers" />

## Products

Go to **Tenants → Products** to see every product across all tenant stores.

<ImagePopup src="/images/tenant-data/super-products.png" alt="All tenant products" />

## Orders

Go to **Tenants → Orders** to see every order across all tenant stores.

<ImagePopup src="/images/tenant-data/super-orders.png" alt="All tenant orders" />

::: warning
These lists are view only. You can browse, search, and filter, but you cannot edit or delete a tenant's records — only the tenant manages their own store data.
:::

::: tip
To act on a specific store rather than browse across all of them, open the tenant from [Tenant Management](/super-admin/tenant-management) and use **Login As Tenant**.
:::
