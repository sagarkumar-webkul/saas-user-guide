# Tenant Billing

A merchant manages their own subscription from the **Billing** menu in their admin panel — reviewing their plan and usage, comparing plans, subscribing, and viewing invoices.

::: info What you'll learn
- How a tenant reads their plan overview and usage
- How a tenant chooses and pays for a plan
- Where a tenant finds invoices and plan history
:::

## Plan Overview

Go to **Billing → Overview** for a snapshot of the current plan: its name and price, next billing date, payment status, and payment method.

<ImagePopup src="/images/tenant-billing/tenant-billing-overview.png" alt="Tenant plan overview" />

**Plan Usage** shows how much of each allowance is used — products, categories, attributes, attribute families, channels, and orders — as a bar against the plan's limit. **Purchased Plan History** below lists every plan the store has been on.

::: warning
When a store gets close to a plan allowance, the usage bar warns the merchant. To keep adding once a limit is reached, the tenant must upgrade to a plan with a higher allowance.
:::

## Choosing a plan

Go to **Billing → Plans** to compare every available plan side by side, each with its price and feature list. Toggle between **Monthly** and **Yearly** pricing; the current plan is marked. Select **Choose** on a plan to start subscribing.

<ImagePopup src="/images/tenant-billing/tenant-plans.png" alt="Choose a subscription plan" />

At checkout the merchant enters their billing details, picks a payment method — **Stripe**, **PayPal**, or **Manual** — and confirms. With Stripe or PayPal they are redirected to pay securely; with Manual, an invoice is generated instead.

## Invoices

Go to **Billing → Invoices** for the store's own subscription invoices. Click the **View** icon to open one.

<ImagePopup src="/images/tenant-billing/tenant-invoices.png" alt="Tenant subscription invoices" />

## Purchased Plan History

The overview and plans screens show the store's **Purchased Plan History** — every plan it has subscribed to, with the paid amount, payment cycle, and state.

<ImagePopup src="/images/tenant-billing/tenant-plan-history.png" alt="Purchased plan history" />

::: tip
The plan a tenant is on also decides which [storefront themes](/tenants/storefront-themes) they can use — upgrading to a plan with premium themes unlocks the premium designs.
:::
