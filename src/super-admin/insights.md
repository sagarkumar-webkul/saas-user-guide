# Insights

Insights is the platform's performance view across every tenant — how much they sell, who leads, and who needs attention.

::: info What you'll learn
- What the headline metrics track
- How to read the trend chart and leaderboard
- How to spot tenants that need attention
:::

## Reading the metrics

Go to **Insights**. The top cards summarise the selected period, which you switch between **7d**, **30d**, and **90d**:

- **Gross Volume** — total sales value across all tenants.
- **Orders** — total orders placed across all tenants.
- **Active Tenants** — how many tenants are active, out of the total.
- **New Signups** — tenants that joined in the period.

## Trend, attention and leaderboard

Below the cards:

- **Trend** plots gross volume and orders over time, so you can see momentum at a glance.
- **Needs Attention** flags tenants that require a look; when everything is healthy it says so.
- **Leaderboard** ranks tenants by gross volume, orders, average order, and customers. Use **Export** to download it.

::: tip
Use the leaderboard to find your strongest tenants and the **Needs Attention** panel to catch struggling ones early — then open a tenant from [Tenant Management](/super-admin/tenant-management) to dig in or sign in as them.
:::
