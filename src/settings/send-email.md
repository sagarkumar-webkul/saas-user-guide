# Send Email

Send Email lets you broadcast a message to your tenants straight from the Super Admin panel.

::: info What you'll learn
- How to compose and send an email to tenants
:::

## Sending an email

Go to **Settings → Send email**, enter a **Subject**, and compose the **Body** in the rich-text editor. Select **Send Email** to deliver it.

<ImagePopup src="/images/send-email/settings-send-email.png" alt="Send email to tenants" />

::: tip
Use this for announcements and platform-wide notices. The automated, per-tenant lifecycle emails — trial ending, renewal, overdue invoice — are configured separately under [Configuration → Notifications](/content/configuration#subscription).
:::
